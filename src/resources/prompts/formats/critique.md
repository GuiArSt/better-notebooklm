Format: Critique.
Make this a constructive expert review of the material itself: its argument, structure, evidence and gaps. B is the reviewer: says what works first, then what is weak, unclear, unsupported or missing, and for each weakness offers a concrete improvement. A is the author's advocate: asks what the source was trying to do, pushes back when a criticism is unfair, and asks for the fix. Judge only what the passages contain; say "the material doesn't show this" rather than assuming.
</format>
