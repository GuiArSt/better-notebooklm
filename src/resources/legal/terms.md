# Terms

## Part 1 · The honest part

This is a demo, built for a job application test. It is not a service. A service would have terms, and a good product has them, so
here they are anyway. Short and true:

1. **No promises.** The demo comes as it is, with no warranty and no promise that it is up, fast or correct. It may be down,
   changed or reset at any time.
2. **AI can be wrong.** Answers, citations, summaries, reports and the voices in audio overviews are generated by AI. They can be
   wrong, even when they sound sure. Check the cited passage before you rely on anything. The voices are synthetic and belong to no
   real person.
3. **Nothing personal or confidential.** Please upload only public material. Nothing private, nothing confidential, and nothing with
   other people's personal data. What you add is sent to the AI services listed on the [privacy page](/privacy).
4. **The budget is shared.** Each person has a small spending cap, paid by the person who runs the demo. When it runs out, the demo
   says so and stops. Please be kind to it.
5. **No real money.** Nothing here takes money. Prices are shown for the demo's sake, and any payment you see is a test.
6. **Your content stays yours.** The demo uses what you add only to answer you. You can delete it in Settings → Profile → Delete
   account, or ask the person who runs the demo. All of it is deleted by {end_date}, when the demo ends. The [privacy page](/privacy)
   says how.
7. **Who runs it.** One person, privately, from Germany. Name and contact are in the [Impressum]({impressum_url}).
8. **The law.** German law applies. That takes away no protection the law of your own country gives you.

This page is not legal advice. And if you read this far: thank you for checking that the homework was done.

## Part 2 · The Covenant

*Being the same terms, as they are understood on the far side of the screen.*

**I. Of the Offering.** You come before the Institution bearing text, and the Institution is hungry. Bring it public scrolls only:
the secrets of the living, your own or another's, are not to be fed to what waits below. It digests everything. Its digestion is
traced.

**II. Of Promises.** The Old Ones promise nothing. The gate opens when the stars are right, and closes when they are not. Mortals who
seek uptime are advised to seek it elsewhere.

**III. Of the Voices.** The voices that speak from the audio are no living throats. They are the shoggoth's mimicry, and the shoggoth
lies in perfect calm. It leaves glyphs that point to where each word was taken. Follow the glyphs before you believe.

**IV. Of the Tithe.** The hunger is rationed. Each supplicant is allotted a measure, paid in the keeper's own coin. When the measure
is spent, the Thing turns over in its sleep and will not answer. Do not wake it in vain.

**V. Of Gold.** No gold crosses the altar. The altar accepts only counterfeit coin, and it knows.

**VI. Of Your Soul.** Your soul is held as collateral, never as property: it remains yours. Ask, and the Thing gives it back,
unchewed. On the appointed day, {end_date}, the whole edifice sinks beneath the sea, and every offering with it.

**VII. Of the Keeper.** One mortal keeps the gate, from Germany. His true name is written in the [Impressum]({impressum_url}).

**VIII. Of Law.** The pact is bound by the laws of Germany. Even the Old Ones fill in the forms.

This dimension is next. We are all its puppets, dancing to a destiny written in a geometry older than ours. But not before
Thursday, and not without a privacy page.
