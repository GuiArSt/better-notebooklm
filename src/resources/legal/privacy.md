# Privacy

This is a demo, built for a job application test and open only to people who were invited. It is not a product. This page tells you
plainly what the demo does with your data. It is not legal advice.

## Who is responsible

The person who built and runs this demo, privately, from Germany. His name and contact are in the [Impressum]({impressum_url}) and on
his [privacy page]({datenschutz_url}). Write to him for anything on this page.

## What the demo keeps

- **Your account:** your name, your e-mail address, and your password (stored only as a hash) or your Google account id. It also keeps
  your role, and your two-factor settings if you turn them on.
  - Your e-mail address is used only to match your invitation and to sign you in. The demo sends no e-mail. The person who runs it
    sent you your invitation link himself, from his own mailbox.
- **What you put in:** your notebooks, your sources (pasted text, PDFs you upload, web pages you add), and your notes.
- **What it makes for you:** summaries, reports, audio overviews and their scripts.
- **For security and the budget:**
  - Your sign-in session, which holds your IP address and browser name (user agent). It keeps you signed in and expires two hours
    after your last request, unless you ticked "Remember me".
  - Counters that limit how often the sign-in and invitation pages can be tried. They are keyed by your IP address, and by your
    e-mail address for sign-in. They exist to stop password guessing, and each one lapses within a minute or an hour.
  - A record of each AI call: which model, how much it cost, when. This record holds no text.

**Your chat** with a notebook is kept **only in your own browser**, not on the server. Signing out or clearing the site's data in your
browser removes it.

No advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## Why

- **To run the demo you were invited to try:** signing you in, storing your notebooks, and answering from your sources.
- **To keep it safe and within its budget:** sessions, rate limits, and a spending cap per person.
- **To let the builder see what went wrong:** the traces described below.

## Who else processes your data

To answer you, your text has to be sent to these services. Each one acts for the demo under its own data processing terms.

| Service | What it gets | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Mistral AI** | Your sources and questions, to answer, summarise, search and speak (the free voice) | EU |
| **ElevenLabs** | The script of an audio overview, which is written from your sources, to voice it for testers and pro members. Training on it is switched off | **USA** |
| **Langfuse Cloud** | A trace of every AI call, including your sources and questions as sent to the model. Keyed by a number, never your e-mail | EU (Ireland) |
| **Anthropic** | Your sources and questions, to answer you when your account is on the pro tier (Claude) | **USA** |
| **OpenAI** | To draw pictures: the title of a new notebook (for its icon) and, for testers and pro members who make an infographic, a short brief written from that notebook's sources (a title and 4 to 6 points). Never the sources themselves | **USA** |
| **Sentry** | When something breaks: the kind of error and internal ids, including the number of your account. No e-mail and nothing you wrote. Kept 30 days | EU (Frankfurt) |
| **Cloudflare** | Your IP address and the traffic, on its way to the server | worldwide |
| **Hetzner** | The server that stores everything listed above | Germany (Nuremberg) |
| **Google** | Only if you choose "Continue with Google": Google tells us your name and e-mail address. Google handles your Google account under its own terms | Google's own |

Where data goes to the USA, it does so under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses, as each provider's
terms state.

## Please use public documents

**Please do not upload anything private, and nothing with other people's personal data** (CVs, e-mails, client files). Everything you
add is sent to the services above. Public web pages and published papers are ideal.

## AI-generated content

Answers, summaries, reports and audio overviews are generated by AI and can be wrong. The voices in audio overviews are synthetic and
belong to no real person.

## Cookies and browser storage

The demo stores only what it needs to work. It asks no consent for these because none of them tracks you.

| Name | What it is for |
|---|---|
| session cookie | keeps you signed in |
| `remember_web_…` | only if you tick "Remember me": keeps you signed in until you sign out |
| `XSRF-TOKEN` | protects your forms from forged requests |
| `appearance` (cookie and browser storage) | your light or dark theme |
| `sidebar_state` | whether the sidebar is open |
| `notebook-chat-…` (browser storage) | your chat with each notebook, kept only in your browser |
| `__cf_bm`, `cf_clearance` (may be set by Cloudflare) | telling people from bots |

## How long

- **Your data is kept until you delete it, and no later than {end_date}.** On that date the whole demo and all its data are deleted,
  including the traces at Langfuse and the audio history at ElevenLabs, which are deleted by hand.
- The AI providers keep their own copies of a request for a short time, usually up to 30 days, to watch for abuse.

## Deleting your data, and your other rights

- **Settings → Profile → Delete account** removes your account, your notebooks, sources, notes, generated audio and reports, and your
  invitation, at once.
  - **[if P-1 and P-2 are not merged]** Instead: "**Settings → Profile → Delete account** removes your account and your notebooks. For
    the files and everything else, write to the person who runs the demo, and he deletes it by hand."
- Traces at Langfuse are deleted by hand: ask, and they are removed with the rest.
- You may also ask what is stored about you, have it corrected, get a copy, or object. Write to the contact in the Impressum. You will
  have an answer within a month.
- If you think your data is mishandled, you may complain to a data protection authority, in particular the one where you live.
